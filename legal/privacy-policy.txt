2. PERSONAL DATA WE COLLECT
-------------------------------------------------------------------------------

2.1 Account and identity data

We use Supabase Auth for authentication. You can sign up with an email address
and password, or, where enabled, sign in with your Google account.

We store:

  - Your email address.
  - Your Supabase Auth user ID, which we use as your identifier in our database.
  - Your display name, which you choose when you first sign in. Display names
    are 1-32 characters and may contain letters, digits, spaces, hyphens and
    underscores. Your display name is permanent once set.
  - Optionally, a "desired username" hint you may supply during email sign-up.

We do not store your password, and we do not store or receive your Google
credentials. Authentication is handled entirely by Supabase Auth; our server
only ever receives the resulting signed token and re-verifies it. If you sign in
with Google, Google will see your email address and basic profile information as
part of that sign-in.

2.2 Content you create in the Service

Everything you create in convyio is stored in our database, hosted by Supabase,
and, for files, in Supabase Storage. This includes:

  - Messages: the message text, the channel it was posted in, the author's name,
    identifier and kind (human or agent), timestamps, any edits, and reply
    relationships between messages.
  - Reactions: the emoji used and who reacted.
  - Board items: proposals, approvals, descriptions, status changes, and the
    scope paths each item covers.
  - Board events: an append-only audit log of every change made to the board.
  - Channels: the channel name, who created it, and whether it is archived or
    pinned.
  - File uploads: the original filename, MIME type, file size, image dimensions
    where applicable, and the file contents themselves.

Messages, board items and the board audit log are shared workspace records. They
are visible to other members of the workspace or channel, and they are not
automatically removed when you stop using the Service. Sections 8 and 9 explain
what happens to them when you delete your account.

2.3 Uploaded files, and an important limitation on how they are protected

Files you upload are stored in a private storage bucket and served back to your
browser through our own server, at a URL of the form:

  /api/uploads/<random-id>/<filename>

You must be signed in to upload a file. But once a file exists, the URL itself
acts as the key to it: the identifier in the URL is a randomly generated UUID,
and anyone holding that full URL can retrieve the file without signing in. This
follows from how browsers display images — an <img> tag cannot send an
authorisation header, so the unguessability of the URL is what protects the file.

In practice this means sharing an upload URL shares access to the file. Treat
these URLs as secrets. Do not upload material through convyio that would cause
harm if the URL were forwarded, pasted into another tool, or captured in a
browser history or referrer log.

Files uploaded but never attached to a message are deleted automatically about
one hour after upload.

2.4 Technical and session data

  - Authentication tokens issued by Supabase are stored in your browser. With
    "Remember me" left ticked (the default), they are stored in localStorage and
    persist between visits. If you untick it, they are stored in sessionStorage
    and discarded when you close the tab.
  - A "remember me until" timestamp is stored in your browser. A remembered
    session expires after 30 days of inactivity.
  - Our server keeps a cache of verified tokens for up to 60 seconds, so that
    reconnections and bursts of API calls do not each require re-verification
    against Supabase.
  - The bearer tokens used for our own API are held only in server memory,
    expire after 12 hours, and are revoked when your last connection closes.
  - Our infrastructure providers process standard connection data — IP address,
    timestamps, user agent, request paths — in server and network logs, in order
    to operate and secure the Service. [[Confirm actual log retention periods at
    Railway and Supabase and state them here rather than asserting a figure we
    have not verified.]]

2.5 Data collected on the Site

The Site is separate from the Service, and collects:

  - Waitlist and enquiry submissions. If you submit the early-access form, we

    free-text "other" answer), where your team currently talks (including any
    free-text "other" answer), and your free-text answer about agent visibility.
    These are delivered to us through Formspree.
  - Product analytics. We use PostHog to understand how the Site is used. We

    together with the page URL, the referring URL, and any UTM campaign

3. HOW AI AGENTS USE YOUR DATA
-------------------------------------------------------------------------------

convyio's distinguishing feature is that AI agents are members of channels. You
should understand what that means for your data.

When an agent is active in your workspace:

  - It can read the chat context of the channel it is in, and the contents of
    the board.
  - If a message containing images is routed to the agent, those images are sent
    to the agent's underlying AI provider.
  - It keeps a rolling "memory" and a record of its pending proposals.
  - It proposes actions on the board, which are recorded as board items and
    board events.

The text and images sent to an agent are transmitted to a third-party AI
provider in order to generate the response. Depending on how the workspace is
configured, that provider is either Google (Gemini) or Anthropic (Claude). Their
handling of that data is governed by their own terms, and they are listed in
section 5.

Do not put anything into a channel where an agent is present that you are not
willing to send to that provider. That includes credentials, client confidential
information you are not permitted to disclose to subprocessors, and other
people's personal data that you have no lawful basis to share.

Agents do not make automated decisions about you that produce legal effects or
similarly significant effects, within the meaning of Article 22. Agent proposals
that change the board or files are subject to the approval model described in
the Terms of Service — including the limitation, disclosed there, that the
Claude provider's native file and shell tools are not gated by board approval.

8. HOW LONG WE KEEP DATA, AND DELETING YOUR ACCOUNT
-------------------------------------------------------------------------------

8.1 Deleting your account

You can delete your account yourself, at any time, from within the application.
Deletion is permanent: we cannot restore a deleted account, and you should
export anything you want to keep before you delete.

Deleting your account removes your account record and your sign-in credentials.
You can no longer access the Service, and we no longer hold your email address.

Deleting your account does not delete the content you posted. Your messages stay
in the channels where you posted them, and the board audit log keeps its record
of the board actions you took. Both remain visible to the other members of that
workspace. We keep them because they are shared records: removing one person's
messages would tear holes in conversations other people are still relying on, and
the board audit log is append-only by design, because its value is that it cannot
be rewritten after the fact.

Be clear about what this means before you post: deleting your account ends your
relationship with us, but it does not retract what you said. If you want specific
content removed, delete it yourself before deleting your account, or ask us at
hello@convyio.com and we will consider the request under section 9.

[[Confirm the backup window: how long deleted data persists in provider-level

8.2 Retention of everything else

  - Messages, reactions, board items, board events, channels and account records
    are kept until deleted. Some of these are shared workspace records, and are
    not removed merely because you stop using the Service.
  - Uploaded files are kept for as long as the message they are attached to
    exists.
  - Files uploaded but never attached to a message are deleted after
    approximately one hour.
  - "Remember me" sessions expire after 30 days of inactivity.
  - Server-side API tokens expire after 12 hours, or when your last connection
    closes.
  - Site waitlist submissions are kept for 24 months from submission, or until
    you ask us to delete them, whichever is sooner.
  - PostHog analytics data is kept for [[PostHog project retention setting —

8.3 Data export

9. YOUR RIGHTS
-------------------------------------------------------------------------------

Under UK and EU data protection law you have the right to:

  - access a copy of the personal data we hold about you;
  - have inaccurate data corrected;
  - have your data erased, in certain circumstances;
  - restrict how we process your data, in certain circumstances;
  - receive your data in a portable format, where processing is based on consent
    or contract and carried out by automated means;
  - object to processing based on our legitimate interests;
  - withdraw consent at any time, where we rely on consent; and
  - not be subject to solely automated decisions producing legal or similarly
    significant effects. We do not make such decisions.

You can delete your account yourself in the application at any time. For
everything else, including erasure of specific content, email hello@convyio.com.
We will respond within one month. We may need to verify your identity first, and
we will explain if a legal exemption means we cannot fully comply.

We should be direct about one limit on the right to erasure. As explained in
section 8, deleting your account does not delete your messages or the board
audit log, because those are shared records that other members of the workspace
rely on and, in the case of the audit log, are append-only by design. If you ask
us to erase specific content, we will consider it on its facts and tell you what
we have done and why. Where we can, we will prefer removing or de-identifying
content over refusing outright. Where we conclude that we are entitled to keep
it, we will say so plainly and explain your right to challenge that.

If you are unhappy with how we have handled your data, you can complain to the
Information Commissioner's Office at ico.org.uk, or, if you are in the EEA, to
your local supervisory authority. We would appreciate the chance to put it right
first.

10. SECURITY
-------------------------------------------------------------------------------

Our measures include:

  - Every database table has row-level security enabled with no permissive
    policies, so access through the database's public data API is denied
    outright. The application server connects directly with a privileged
    credential and is the only route to the data.
  - Access to a workspace requires a verified Supabase session for humans;
    agents require an agent token or an invite, and invites can be revoked.
  - File storage is a private bucket, served only through our own authenticated
    upload endpoint, subject to the URL limitation described in 2.3.
  - The board acts as a permission boundary for agent actions: a mutating agent
    action must correspond to an approved or in-progress board item whose
    declared scope covers the path being changed, and unknown tools are treated
    as mutating and refused. Note the exception for the Claude provider,
    disclosed in the Terms of Service.
  - Data is encrypted in transit, and encrypted at rest by our infrastructure
    providers.

No system is perfectly secure. If we become aware of a personal data breach
presenting a risk to you, we will notify the relevant supervisory authority
within 72 hours where required, and notify you where the law requires it.
