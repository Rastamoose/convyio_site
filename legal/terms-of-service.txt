5. AI AGENTS — PLEASE READ THIS SECTION CAREFULLY
-------------------------------------------------------------------------------

5.1 What agents are. An agent is an AI participant in a channel. It reads chat
context and the board, and it can propose and carry out actions, such as creating
and updating board items and, depending on configuration, changing files.

5.2 Third-party providers. Agents are powered by third-party AI services —
Google's Gemini or Anthropic's Claude, depending on configuration. Message text,
board context and any images routed to an agent are sent to that provider. Your
use of agents is therefore also subject to that provider's acceptable use policy,
and we may have to suspend or change agent features if a provider changes its
terms, pricing or availability.

5.3 Output is not reliable. Agent output may be wrong, incomplete, out of date or
fabricated, and may be stated confidently while being wrong. It is not
professional, legal, financial, medical or engineering advice. You must review
agent output before relying on it or acting on it. You remain responsible for
decisions you or your team take, whether or not an agent suggested them.

5.4 The approval model, and its limits. convyio is designed so that an agent's
changes are gated: a mutating action must correspond to a board item that a human
has approved, whose declared scope covers the thing being changed, and actions
that are not known to be read-only are refused by default.

  IMPORTANT LIMITATION: where a workspace is configured to use the Claude
  provider, that provider's own file-writing, file-editing and shell command
  tools operate alongside the board tools and are NOT gated by board approval.
  In that configuration, an agent can change files and run commands without a
  human approving a board item first.

If you enable an agent, you accept this. Before you do, assume the agent may
modify or delete files and run commands in the environment you have given it
access to, and accordingly:

  - only give an agent access to an environment where that is acceptable;
  - keep version control and independent backups of anything it can reach;
  - never place credentials, secrets or production access in an agent-accessible
    environment; and
  - supervise agent activity rather than leaving it unattended.

To the fullest extent permitted by law, we are not liable for loss, corruption or
unintended disclosure of data, or for other consequences, arising from actions
taken by an agent you enabled. This does not limit our liability for the matters
listed in clause 10.1.

[COUNSEL NOTE: This clause is the main reason we wanted a lawyer involved early.
